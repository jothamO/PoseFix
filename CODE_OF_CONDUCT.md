# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want PoseFix to be a useful, welcoming open-source project. Be respectful, constructive, and specific when discussing code, research, photography, people, and generated images.

Examples of welcome behavior include giving actionable feedback, assuming good faith, crediting others' work, and keeping technical disagreements focused on the work.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, deliberate humiliation, doxxing, or sharing private material without permission are not acceptable.

## Image-specific expectations

Do not publish private photographs, identifying personal information, or intimate material without appropriate permission. Test images in issues and pull requests should be synthetic, self-owned, or properly licensed.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and participation that violate these standards. Serious concerns should be reported privately using the process in `SECURITY.md` rather than escalated publicly.
